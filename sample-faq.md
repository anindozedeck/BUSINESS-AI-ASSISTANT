# Sample business FAQ

This fictional FAQ lets the assistant run as an example. Replace it with
accurate, approved information for your business before serving customers.

## Orders and delivery

Customers can request order updates by contacting support with their order
number. Delivery availability and estimated times depend on the customer's
location and product. Confirm current estimates with the business before
promising a delivery date.

## Returns

For return eligibility, time limits, and refund status, contact the business's
support team with the order number and purchase date. This sample does not
define a return window or guarantee that a product can be returned.

## Product information

Ask the support team to confirm product specifications, current prices, and
stock before placing an order. This example does not contain a real product
catalog or current prices.
